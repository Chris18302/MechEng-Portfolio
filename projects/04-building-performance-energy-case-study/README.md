## Situation / Engineering Problem

Improving building energy performance requires more than selecting efficient equipment. Engineers and technicians need to understand the building envelope, HVAC systems, operating conditions, utility use, retrofit opportunities, and the policy requirements that drive upgrades.

## Key Technical Connections

### Envelope + HVAC
Reducing loads through weatherization and envelope improvements can change equipment-sizing and operating requirements.

### Heat Pumps + Electrification
Heat-pump selection depends on application, source/sink conditions, distribution systems, climate, and existing-building constraints—not simply efficiency ratings.

### Benchmarking + Action
Energy reporting and benchmarking create visibility, but engineering value comes from using data to identify and prioritize actual improvements.

### Policy + Engineering
NYC energy requirements influence what owners measure, audit, upgrade, and report, connecting engineering analysis directly to capital planning and decarbonization.
