# Residential Radiator Retrofit — Field Engineering Case Study

## Project Context
Supported residential heating-retrofit work involving custom smart radiator enclosures and temperature sensing in occupied NYC buildings.

## My Contributions
- Reviewed mechanical/plumbing plans to locate radiators before field work.
- Measured existing radiators and selected enclosure sizes based on field dimensions.
- Installed custom enclosures and modified components when site conditions required adjustment.
- Installed sensors used to monitor radiator inlet, outlet, and ambient temperature.
- Worked around one-pipe and two-pipe steam heating systems.
- Coordinated with tenants, superintendents, property managers, and installation crews.
- Supported inventory, material preparation, quality checks, and equipment reuse.

## Engineering Relevance
This work strengthened my understanding of the gap between drawings and real field conditions. Retrofit work requires measurement accuracy, constructability awareness, safe installation practices, communication, and the ability to adapt a standard product to existing building conditions.

## Skills Demonstrated
Steam heating · HVAC retrofit exposure · field measurement · temperature sensors · mechanical assembly · installation QA/QC · field coordination · bilingual communication
