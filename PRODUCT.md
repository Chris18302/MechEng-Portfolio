# Product

## Register
brand

## Audience
Mechanical, MEP, HVAC, commissioning, controls, building-performance, and energy-engineering hiring managers; engineering recruiters; project managers; and technical peers reviewing Christopher Suarez's work.

## Purpose
Make Christopher's engineering direction obvious within 10 seconds, then prove it with field experience, technical analysis, and project work. The portfolio should connect real building-system exposure to engineering fundamentals and show a clear progression toward HVAC design, commissioning, BAS/controls, and energy engineering.

## Core message
Christopher is a Mechanical Engineering Technology student who already understands the field side of building systems and is deliberately building the calculation, instrumentation, controls, and design skills needed for entry-level mechanical/energy roles.

## Brand personality
Technical, grounded, curious, disciplined, practical.

Three-word personality: **technical, field-tested, deliberate**.

## Content priorities
1. Engineering work and project evidence before biography.
2. HVAC / MEP / energy / commissioning focus before general software skills.
3. Real responsibilities and measurable technical details over buzzwords.
4. Sanitized employer case studies that protect confidential information.
5. Clear “building next” section that shows active skill development.

## Anti-references
Avoid:
- Generic software-developer portfolio styling.
- Neon gradients, glassmorphism, glowing blobs, or “AI portfolio” visuals.
- Dense grids of identical cards.
- Skill-logo walls with no evidence of application.
- Long autobiography before project work.
- Inflated claims about responsibilities or software proficiency.
- Employer-sensitive drawings, tenant information, addresses, or proprietary project data.

## Accessibility
- WCAG-oriented contrast.
- Semantic HTML.
- Keyboard-visible focus states.
- Responsive down to mobile widths.
- Reduced-motion preference respected.
- No interaction required to access core content.
