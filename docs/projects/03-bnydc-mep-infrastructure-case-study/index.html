P-03 MEP / Infrastructure

Project context

I supported operations and capital-project teams working on utilities, mechanical systems, restoration, and infrastructure projects across the Brooklyn Navy Yard.

My contributions

  • Drafted underground utility layouts in AutoCAD.
  • Conducted field observations of underground piping, steam-distribution systems, and mechanical equipment.
  • Reviewed mechanical/HVAC drawings for steam-line relocation, water piping, PRV/backflow vaults, and restoration or mitigation work.
  • Compared Resident Engineer information and field conditions with drawings and as-built documentation.
  • Supported RFIs, RFPs, IFBs, submittals, bid forms, and change orders.
  • Coordinated information across operations, finance, leasing, property management, and Design & Construction teams.
  • Developed and presented a self-initiated overview of NYCECC and Special Inspections.

Engineering relevance

This experience developed my ability to read engineering drawings, verify field conditions, identify documentation discrepancies, understand project delivery, and communicate technical information across multiple stakeholders.
