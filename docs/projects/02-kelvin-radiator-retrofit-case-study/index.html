P-02 HVAC Retrofit / Field Engineering

Project context

I supported residential heating-retrofit work involving custom smart radiator enclosures and temperature sensing in occupied NYC buildings.

My contributions

  • Reviewed mechanical and plumbing plans to locate radiators before field work.
  • Measured existing radiators and selected enclosure sizes from field dimensions.
  • Installed custom enclosures and modified components when actual site conditions differed from expectations.
  • Installed sensors used to monitor radiator inlet, outlet, and ambient temperature.
  • Worked around one-pipe and two-pipe steam heating systems.
  • Coordinated with tenants, superintendents, property managers, and installation crews.
  • Supported inventory, material preparation, quality checks, and equipment reuse.

Engineering relevance

The work strengthened my understanding of the gap between engineering drawings and real field conditions. Retrofit work depends on measurement accuracy, constructability, safe installation practices, communication, and the ability to adapt standard equipment to existing buildings.
